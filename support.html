Frequently asked

I don't hear any sound.
Make sure your iPhone's volume is up and the ring/silent switch on the left edge is set to ring (no orange showing). Tap any chord to start the audio.
What does ChordLoop Pro include?
A one-time $3.99 purchase unlocks MIDI export, drum grooves, and six additional instruments (Organ, Nylon Guitar, Synth, Strings, Music Box, and Marimba). The full songwriting core is free.
I bought Pro on another device / reinstalled the app.
Open the Pro screen and tap “Restore Purchases.” Your unlock is tied to your Apple ID and restores for free.
Does it work offline?
Yes. ChordLoop works fully offline and never sends any data over the internet.
Do you collect any of my data?
No. ChordLoop collects nothing — see our Privacy Policy.

ChordLoop — Privacy Policy

Effective July 9, 2026

ChordLoop is designed to respect your privacy completely. The app does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information.

What we collect

Nothing. Specifically:

  • No account or sign-in is required to use the app.
  • We do not collect analytics, usage data, or diagnostics.
  • We do not access your contacts, location, microphone, photos, or files.
  • Chord progressions you save are stored only on your device, never uploaded.
  • The app works fully offline and does not send any data over the internet.
  • We do not use third-party advertising, tracking, or analytics services.

In-app purchase

ChordLoop offers one optional in-app purchase, “ChordLoop Pro.” Purchases and restores are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment details, and buying Pro collects no personal information within the app.

Your choices

Because ChordLoop collects no data, there is nothing to access, correct, export, or delete on our side. Removing the app from your device removes any progressions you saved.

Children

ChordLoop is safe for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
